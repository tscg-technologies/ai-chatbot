- **Image Number:** `#1`
- **Exact Filename:** `ai-chatbot-hero.webp`
- **Page and Section:** `index.html` — Hero Section (`Hero Visual Wrapper`)
- **Image Subject and Purpose:** Abstract 3D conversational AI core node linking speech bubble glass panels across web, mobile messaging, and voice streams to reinforce omnichannel chatbot architecture.
- **Aspect Ratio:** `21:9` (compatible with `16:9` crop)
- **Recommended Resolution:** `1920 × 820 px`
- **Orientation:** Landscape
- **Composition and Camera Angle:** Wide cinematic isometric angle (`30-degree` elevated perspective), centered focal node with balanced left and right data streams.
- **Lighting:** Soft volumetric rim lighting in electric blue and cyan, subtle specular reflections on frosted dark glass surfaces.
- **Background:** Deep midnight navy (`#080B16`) blending into dark slate (`#10172A`) with a faint geometric dot grid.

- **Visual Style:** Premium futuristic SaaS 3D UI illustration, clean glassmorphism, minimal abstract geometry.
- **Subject Positioning:** Central glowing speech-bubble AI node in the middle third, connected via fine fiber-optic light lines to three smaller floating channel cards.
- **Negative Space Requirements:** Keep 25% breathing room along top and outer edges so the visual integrates cleanly into the dark card frame.

**Complete Google Flow Generation Prompt:**
```text
A premium futuristic SaaS 3D illustration of an omnichannel conversational AI network hub, wide 21:9 composition, 30-degree isometric camera angle. In the center, a translucent frosted dark-glass speech bubble node with three glowing interconnected neural dots inside it, radiating soft electric blue (#5B8CFF) and cyan (#36D9E8) light.
```
